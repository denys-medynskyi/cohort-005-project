# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy courses and work through their lessons.

## Language

### People

**Learner**:
A person who buys courses and works through their lessons.
_Avoid_: Student, user, customer

**Admin**:
A member of the Cadence team who runs the platform, including deciding Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: support, staff

### Getting money back

**Refund Request**:
A Learner's request, with a written reason, to give a purchase back and get their money returned. An Admin approves or declines it. A purchase can have only one Refund Request.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation, pending chargeback

**Pending** / **Approved** / **Declined**:
The states of a Refund Request: waiting for a decision, accepted, or turned down. Approved and Declined are final.
_Avoid_: Requested, Open

**Refund**:
Money returned to a Learner once their Refund Request is approved. Approval also ends the Learner's access to the course.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Refund Amount**:
How much a Refund returns. It is always the amount the Learner paid for the purchase.
_Avoid_: refundable amount, list price

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen calendar days after a purchase, the last day included, during which a Learner may raise a Refund Request.
_Avoid_: cancel window

**Refund Eligibility**:
The conditions a purchase must meet before a Refund Request can be raised: it is within the Refund Window, the Learner has watched under two hours and completed under half the lessons, and it is not a claimed team seat.
_Avoid_: cancellation offer
