# Cadence

An online course platform where learners buy, watch, and complete courses.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, against one of their Purchases, to have their money sent back. It is reviewed by a person before any money moves.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Cancellation, cancel my purchase

**Refund**:
The money actually sent back to the learner once a Refund Request is Approved.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback

**Refund Window**:
The fourteen whole calendar days after a Purchase during which a Refund Request may be made.
_Avoid_: Cancel window, cancellation period

**Refund Eligibility**:
The conditions a Purchase must meet for a Refund Request to be made: inside the Refund Window, under two hours of video watched, under half the lessons completed, not a claimed team seat, and no Refund Request already Requested.
_Avoid_: Cancellation offer, cancellable

**Refundable Amount**:
What a Refund Request is for: the amount the learner paid for the Purchase.
_Avoid_: Reversal amount

**Requested**:
A Refund Request waiting on a person to review it.
_Avoid_: Pending, open

**Approved**:
A Refund Request a person accepted, so a Refund is sent.

**Declined**:
A Refund Request a person turned down, so no money moves.
_Avoid_: Rejected
